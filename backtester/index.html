How fills work

Entries fill at the next bar's open plus half the spread and slippage. If one 1-minute bar contains both your stop and target, it resolves as a stop and is flagged ambiguous — 1m data cannot say which came first, and assuming the winner is how manual backtests flatter themselves. Auto-breakeven (if enabled) arms at the end of the bar that reaches the trigger, so it can never rescue a trade inside the same bar. Commission is charged per contract per side.
