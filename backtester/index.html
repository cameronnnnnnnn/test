How fills are simulated

Entries fill at the next bar's open plus half the spread and your slippage setting. If a single 1-minute bar's range contains both your stop and your target, the trade is resolved as a stop-out and flagged ambiguous in the trade log — 1-minute data cannot tell which came first, and assuming the winner is how manual backtests flatter themselves. Commission is charged per contract per side.
