Why: the one real drift is off-limits

On the Nasdaq, the reliably positive drift is overnight — roughly half the total move and historically the larger half. Topstep's no-overnight rule bars us from harvesting it. The tradeable RTH drift is smaller, flips sign by regime (strongly negative in 2022), and is indistinguishable from noise net of costs. This is a structural ceiling, not a tuning failure.

If you attempt it anyway

  • Size small: ~2–3 MNQ, 25–40 pt stop, trend-filtered direction, flat by 3:10 CT. Maximizes the ~30% low-ruin odds; minimizes a Phase-1 ($50k→$52k) trailing stop-out.
  • Don't scale up for speed. Bigger size only raises blow-up; it never lifts pass% above the noise ceiling.
  • Treat a pass as luck, not income. A funded account then faces the same ~30% odds every payout cycle — the underlying process has no edge.
